Four Schools of Excellence

NAFCOL is organised into four specialised schools, each delivering world-class professional training tailored to modern logistics operations.

SLSM

School of Logistics & Supply Management

Procurement, inventory control, distribution networks, and integrated supply chain strategy for defence and civilian practitioners.
