About NAFCOL

Discover our history, vision, and the leadership driving excellence in logistics education across Nigeria and West Africa.

Shaping Logistics Leaders Since 1964

The Nigerian Air Force College of Logistics (NAFCOL) is Nigeria's premier institution dedicated to the professional training of logistics and supply chain practitioners within the defence sector and beyond.

Established to meet the growing demand for skilled logistics professionals capable of supporting Nigeria's national defence and economic development, NAFCOL has evolved over six decades into a world-class centre of excellence recognised across West Africa.

Today, our four schools deliver comprehensive programmes accredited by the Federal Ministry of Education and aligned with international benchmarks in logistics, supply chain management, engineering services, catering, and specialised defence logistics operations.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To become a world-class professional training, learning and experience centre in logistics specializations, serving as a reference point for excellence in the region.

Our Mission

To deliberately build the capacity of logistics practitioners and serve as a synergy hub for collaboration and innovation in logistics management across Nigeria and the African continent.

Core Values

Service

Dedicated service to Nigeria and the Nigerian Air Force is at the heart of everything we do. We train professionals who lead with commitment and integrity.

Support

We provide robust support structures that enable both students and graduates to excel — from world-class facilities to an active alumni network.

Sustainability

We build capabilities that endure beyond programmes. Our graduates go on to create lasting impact in their organisations and communities.

Excellence

We hold ourselves and our graduates to the highest professional standards, consistently raising the bar for logistics education in Nigeria.

Innovation

Continuously evolving our curriculum and methods to reflect the latest advances in global logistics, technology, and supply chain management.

Collaboration

NAFCOL serves as a synergy hub — connecting military and civilian practitioners, government institutions, and international partners.
